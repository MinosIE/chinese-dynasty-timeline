# Chinese Dynasties · A Timeline of Three Millennia

> A static, zero-dependency educational site on Chinese imperial history from the Xia to the Qing dynasty (18 dynasties). Full plain-text dump of all structured data for LLM ingestion.

Source: https://MinosIE.github.io/chinese-dynasty-timeline/

## 各王朝详情

## Xia（c. 2070–1600 BC）
- 大时代：Pre-Qin
- 都城：Yangcheng (legendary)
- 特征：The first hereditary dynasty in legend; the “family rule” began here. Major rulers per the Bamboo Annals, ~470 yrs.
- 总结：Historicity still debated, cross-checked with Erlitou archaeology; marks the dawn of early statehood in the Central Plains.

### 帝王
- Yu (Da Yu)（Founding）：c. 2070–2025 BC，在位约 45 年。Tamed the floods and divided the land into nine provinces; founded the Xia
- Qi（Second）：c. 2025–1996 BC，在位约 29 年。Established hereditary rule; the “family rule” began
- Taikang：c. 1996–1978 BC，在位约 18 年。Lost the state; power slipped away
- Zhongkang：c. 1978–1965 BC，在位约 13 年。A puppet; real power with Houyi
- Xiang：c. 1965–1937 BC，在位约 28 年。Killed by Hanzhuo
- Shaokang：c. 1937–1915 BC，在位约 22 年。The Shaokang Restoration; recovered the state
- Zhu：c. 1915–1898 BC，在位约 17 年。Campaigns against the Eastern Yi; Xia resurged
- Huai：c. 1898–1872 BC，在位约 26 年。The Nine Yi came to court
- Mang：c. 1872–1854 BC，在位约 18 年。Offered river sacrifices to the Yellow River
- Xie：c. 1854–1838 BC，在位约 16 年。Vassals submitted
- Bu Jiang：c. 1838–1779 BC，在位约 59 年。Longest reign; the Xia house was strong
- Jiong：c. 1779–1758 BC，在位约 21 年。Maintained the legacy
- Jin：c. 1758–1737 BC，在位约 21 年。Xia in gradual decline
- Kong Jia：c. 1737–1706 BC，在位约 31 年。Fond of spirits; vassals rebelled
- Gao：c. 1706–1695 BC，在位约 11 年。An age of decline
- Fa：c. 1695–1676 BC，在位约 19 年。An age of decline
- Jie (Lügui)（Last ruler）：c. 1676–1600 BC，在位约 76 年，谥号 Jie。Tyrannical and unjust; Xia fell

### 人才
- Others：Houyi（Leader of the Eastern Yi; usurped Xia）；Hanzhuo（Killed Houyi and seized power）

### 制度
- Hereditary rule replaced abdication
- Channeling waters to control floods
- The “family rule” concept established

### 三维（政治 · 经济 · 文化）
- Politics：Hereditary rule replaced abdication; the “family rule” was thereby established；Kingship took shape; the tribal league transitioned toward an early state
- Economy：Farm-based; Yu's flood control is said to have aided irrigation；The Erlitou culture shows bronze casting and a division of handicraft labor
- Culture：No mature writing yet; history intertwined with legend；Ritual order and calendar took root (the Xia Xiaozheng is said to be the Xia calendar)

## Shang（1600–1046 BC）
- 大时代：Pre-Qin
- 都城：Yin (present-day Anyang)
- 特征：Zenith of oracle-bone script and bronze civilization, with theocratic and royal power fused. Major rulers per the traditional line, ~554 yrs.
- 总结：Oracle bones confirm the Shang royal genealogy — the earliest dynasty with contemporaneous written records in China.

- Tang (Tian Yi)（Founding）：c. 1600–1588 BC，在位约 12 年，庙号 Taizu。Destroyed Xia, founded Shang
- Wai Bing：c. 1588–1586 BC，在位约 2 年。Yi Yin as regent
- Zhong Ren：c. 1586–1582 BC，在位约 4 年。Yi Yin as regent
- Tai Jia：c. 1582–1570 BC，在位约 12 年，庙号 Taizong。Yi Yin exiled Tai Jia; he repented and restored order
- Wo Ding：c. 1570–1541 BC，在位约 29 年。Yi Yin died
- Tai Geng：c. 1541–1516 BC，在位约 25 年。Vassals were submissive
- Xiao Jia：c. 1516–1499 BC，在位约 17 年。Maintained the legacy
- Yong Ji：c. 1499–1487 BC，在位约 12 年。Vassals rebelled
- Tai Wu：c. 1487–1412 BC，在位约 75 年，庙号 Zhongzong。The Tai Wu Restoration; Shang resurged
- Zhong Ding：c. 1412–1401 BC，在位约 11 年。Moved to Ao
- Wai Ren：c. 1401–1386 BC，在位约 15 年。The Disorder of the Nine Generations began
- He Dan Jia：c. 1386–1377 BC，在位约 9 年。Moved to Xiang
- Zu Yi：c. 1377–1358 BC，在位约 19 年，庙号 Zhongzong。Moved to Xing; Yin revived
- Zu Xin：c. 1358–1342 BC，在位约 16 年。Maintained the legacy
- Wo Jia：c. 1342–1317 BC，在位约 25 年。Maintained the legacy
- Zu Ding：c. 1317–1285 BC，在位约 32 年。Maintained the legacy
- Nan Geng：c. 1285–1260 BC，在位约 25 年。Moved to Yan
- Yang Jia：c. 1260–1253 BC，在位约 7 年。Vassals weakened
- Pan Geng：c. 1253–1225 BC，在位约 28 年。Moved to Yin, stabilizing politics
- Xiao Xin：c. 1225–1204 BC，在位约 21 年。Yin declined again
- Xiao Yi：c. 1204–1176 BC，在位约 28 年。Maintained the legacy
- Wu Ding：c. 1176–1117 BC，在位约 59 年，庙号 Gaozong。The Wu Ding Restoration; the state at its zenith
- Zu Geng：c. 1117–1106 BC，在位约 11 年。Maintained the legacy
- Zu Jia：c. 1106–1073 BC，在位约 33 年。Reformed the ritual system
- Lin Xin：c. 1073–1067 BC，在位约 6 年。Waged campaigns
- Di Xin (Zhou Wang)（Last ruler）：c. 1067–1046 BC，在位约 21 年，谥号 Zhou。Tyrannical; fell at the Battle of Muye

### 人才
- Civil Officials：Yi Yin（Founding chancellor; exiled Tai Jia）；Fu Yue（Wise chancellor of Wu Ding）
- Others：Fu Hao（A woman general who led many campaigns）；Bigan（A loyal minister; disemboweled）

### 制度
- Oracle-bone divination and record-keeping
- The bronze ritual-vessel system
- The inner and outer service system

### 三维（政治 · 经济 · 文化）
- Politics：Kingship and theocracy fused; all matters decided by divination；The inner-outer service system; the royal domain governed the four quarters
- Economy：Highly developed bronze casting (e.g., the Simuwu ding)；Agriculture and herding side by side; cowries appeared as primitive money
- Culture：Oracle-bone script is China's earliest mature writing system；Bronze ritual vessels and ancestor worship at their height

## Zhou（Western Zhou 1046–771 BC / Eastern Zhou 770–475 BC (Spring & Autumn)）
- 大时代：Pre-Qin
- 都城：Haojing → Luoyi
- 特征：Founded feudalism, kinship hierarchy and ritual music; after Ping Wang moved east the royal house declined — the Spring & Autumn hegemons vied, the Hundred Schools germinated. Major kings (posthumous names), ~571 yrs.
- 总结：The bedrock of ritual-music civilization; the Spring & Autumn upheaval nurtured the Hundred Schools.

- King Cheng：1042–1021 BC，在位约 22 年，谥号 Cheng。The Duke of Zhou as regent; the Cheng–Kang era of good rule
- King Kang：1020–996 BC，在位约 25 年，谥号 Kang。The Cheng–Kang era; punishments unused
- King Zhao：995–977 BC，在位约 19 年，谥号 Zhao。Lost on the southern campaign
- King Mu：976–922 BC，在位约 55 年，谥号 Mu。Tour to the west, met the Queen Mother; Zhou at its peak
- King Gong：922–900 BC，在位约 23 年，谥号 Gong。Given to hunting
- King Yi：899–892 BC，在位约 8 年，谥号 Yi。The royal house declined
- King Xiao：891–886 BC，在位约 6 年，谥号 Xiao。Succeeded though not the heir
- King Yi：885–878 BC，在位约 8 年，谥号 Yi。Vassals came to court
- King Li：877–841 BC，在位约 37 年，谥号 Li。Monopoly and tyranny; the commoner revolt
- Gonghe (regency)：841–828 BC，在位约 14 年。The Dukes of Zhou and Shao jointly governed; the Gonghe regency begins the chronicle
- King Xuan：827–782 BC，在位约 46 年，谥号 Xuan。The Xuan Restoration
- King You（End of Western Zhou）：781–771 BC，在位约 11 年，谥号 You。Beacon fires to amuse the lords; Western Zhou fell
- King Ping（Start of Eastern Zhou）：770–720 BC，在位约 51 年，谥号 Ping。Moved east to Luoyi; Eastern Zhou began
- King Huan：719–697 BC，在位约 23 年，谥号 Huan。Defeated by Zheng at Xuge
- King Zhuang：696–682 BC，在位约 15 年，谥号 Zhuang。Duke Huan of Qi began his hegemony
- King Xi：679–677 BC，在位约 3 年，谥号 Xi。Duke Huan of Qi asserted hegemony
- King Hui：676–652 BC，在位约 25 年，谥号 Hui。Contending hegemons
- King Xiang：651–619 BC，在位约 33 年，谥号 Xiang。Duke Wen of Jin honored the king
- King Qing：618–613 BC，在位约 6 年，谥号 Qing。Royal house in distress, sought funeral aid
- King Kuang：612–607 BC，在位约 6 年，谥号 Kuang。Maintained the legacy
- King Ding：606–586 BC，在位约 21 年，谥号 Ding。King Zhuang of Chu inquired about the cauldrons
- King Jian：585–572 BC，在位约 14 年，谥号 Jian。Vassals convened alliances
- King Ling：571–545 BC，在位约 27 年，谥号 Ling。Year of Confucius's birth
- King Jing：544–520 BC，在位约 25 年，谥号 Jing。Cast large coins and bells
- King Dao：520 BC，在位约 1 年，谥号 Dao。Reigned just over a year
- King Jing：519–476 BC，在位约 44 年，谥号 Jing。End of Spring and Autumn; Prince Chao's revolt
- King Yuan：475–469 BC，在位约 7 年，谥号 Yuan。The Warring States began
- King Zhending：468–441 BC，在位约 28 年，谥号 Zhending。The three clans destroyed Zhibo
- King Kao：440–426 BC，在位约 15 年，谥号 Kao。Enfeoffed his brother in Henan
- King Weilie：425–402 BC，在位约 24 年，谥号 Weilie。Recognized Wei, Zhao and Han as vassals
- King An：401–376 BC，在位约 26 年，谥号 An。The Tian clan replaced Qi
- King Lie：375–369 BC，在位约 6 年，谥号 Lie。Wei and Qi took the royal title
- King Xian：368–321 BC，在位约 48 年，谥号 Xian。Shang Yang's reform; Qin grew strong
- King Shenjing：320–315 BC，在位约 6 年，谥号 Shenjing。Five states mutually granted kingship
- King Nan（End of Eastern Zhou）：314–256 BC，在位约 59 年，谥号 Nan。Eastern Zhou fell; Qin rose

### 人才
- Civil Officials：Duke of Zhou（Established rites and music; founded the Zhou system）；Guan Zhong（Prime minister of Qi; “revere the king, repel the barbarians”）；Baili Xi（Worthy minister of Duke Mu of Qin）
- Generals：Jiang Ziya (the Grand Duke)（Progenitor of the military art; aided King Wu）
- Thinkers & Men of Letters：Confucius（Founder of Confucianism）；Laozi（Founder of Daoism）；Sun Wu（Sage of war; The Art of War）

### 制度
- The enfeoffment system
- The lineage (clan) system
- The ritual and music system
- The well-field system
- Spring and Autumn hegemony

### 三维（政治 · 经济 · 文化）
- Politics：Enfeoffment, lineage and ritual-music formed a trinity；Eastern Zhou kingship waned; Spring–Autumn hegemony and Warring-States reforms
- Economy：The well-field system prevailed; later iron tools and ox-plough spread；Handicrafts and commerce rose; cities grew
- Culture：The Hundred Schools contended, shaping two millennia of thought；Canons such as the Poetry, Documents and Changes took shape；The Warring States already had the pointing tool “Sinan” (forerunner of the compass)

## Warring States（475–221 BC）
- 大时代：Pre-Qin
- 都城：No single capital (seven contending states)
- 特征：Seven powers vied — Qi, Chu, Yan, Han, Zhao, Wei, Qin; reforms and vertical/horizontal alliances; the Hundred Schools peaked, with Confucian, Daoist, Mohist and Legalist thought shining. Major thinkers, ~254 yrs.
- 总结：An age of contention; institutions and thought erupted together, setting the stage for Qin–Han unification.

### 人才
- Thinkers & Men of Letters：Mencius（Second sage of Confucianism; argued for innate goodness and benevolent rule）；Zhuangzi（Daoist representative; ease and the equality of things）；Mozi（Mohism; universal love and anti-aggression）；Han Fei（Synthesizer of Legalism; central authority）；Xunzi（Between Confucian and Legalist; theory of evil nature）；Zou Yan（Yin–Yang school; cycle of the five virtues）；Gongsun Long（Logicians; “a white horse is not a horse”）
- Civil Officials：Shang Yang（Reformed Qin; rewarded farming and war）；Li Kui（Reformed Wei; farmed the land fully）；Wu Qi（Reformed Chu; strengthened the military）
- Generals：Sun Bin（Military strategist; “besiege Wei to rescue Zhao”）
- Others：Qu Yuan（Father of Chu ci; the Li Sao）；Li Bing（Dujiangyan; blessed Shu with irrigation）

### 制度
- Reform to strengthen
- Vertical–horizontal alliances
- Merit-based nobility
- Seeds of the commandery-county system

### 三维（政治 · 经济 · 文化）
- Politics：Seven powers vied; reform strengthened; merit titles replaced hereditary ones；Vertical and horizontal alliances — diplomacy decided rise and fall
- Economy：Iron tools and ox-plough spread; the well-field fell; smallholder economy rose；Diverse coinage; commerce and cities flourished
- Culture：The Hundred Schools contended: Confucian, Daoist, Mohist, Legalist, Yin–Yang, Logicians；Chu ci and the prose of the philosophers reached a literary peak

## Qin（221–207 BC）
- 大时代：Qin–Han
- 都城：Xianyang
- 特征：China's first unified centralized empire; its institutions shaped two millennia.
- 总结：Brief yet it established the imperial framework; the centralized paradigm endured for two thousand years; after Qin fell the Chu–Han contention ensued, then Han succeeded.

### 帝王
- Qin Shi Huang (Ying Zheng)（First Emperor）：221–210 BC，在位约 11 年，谥号 First Emperor。Conquered the six states and claimed the imperial title
- Qin Ershi (Hu Hai)（Second Emperor）：210–207 BC，在位约 3 年。Tyranny led to a swift fall
- Ziying（Last ruler）：207 BC，在位约 1 年。Reigned 46 days, surrendered to Liu Bang

### 人才
- Civil Officials：Li Si（Chancellor; commanderies-counties system and unified writing）；Zhao Gao（Usurped power; “pointing at a deer and calling it a horse”）
- Generals：Wang Jian（Famed general who destroyed Chu）；Meng Tian（Struck the Xiongnu in the north, built the Great Wall）

### 制度
- Commanderies and counties
- Unified writing, carts and tracks
- Unified weights, measures and coinage
- Built the Great Wall and highways
- Burning of books and burying of scholars
- Governing by law as the teaching

### 三维（政治 · 经济 · 文化）
- Politics：Founded the imperial system, the Three Excellencies and Nine Ministers, and commanderies-counties；The centralized system was thereby established
- Economy：Unified weights, measures, coinage and axle gauges；Built highways and the Lingqu Canal; emphasized agriculture, restrained commerce
- Culture：Unified writing; standardized the script in small seal script；Burning books and burying scholars; thought and culture under tight control

## Chu–Han Contention（206–202 BC）
- 大时代：Qin–Han
- 都城：Pengcheng (Chu) / Yueyang–Chang'an (Han)
- 特征：After Qin fell, Xiang Yu and Liu Bang fought for the realm — the Feast at Hong Gate, the encirclement at Gaixia — settled in four years when Liu Bang founded Han. Major figures, 4 yrs.
- 总结：Commoner Liu Bang defeated Xiang Yu, opening an era of officials from humble origins.

### 人才
- Others：Liu Bang（Founder of Han; a commoner who rose）；Xiang Yu（Hegemon-King of Western Chu; mighty）；Han Xin（“God of War”; “the more the better”）；Zhang Liang（Master strategist; planned within the tent）；Xiao He（Chancellor; secured the state and people）；Chen Ping（Won Liu's cause with stratagems）；Peng Yue（Progenitor of guerrilla war; King of Liang）；Ying Bu（King of Jiujiang; fierce general）

### 制度
- Three Articles of Law
- Enfeoff the vassal kings

## Han（Western Han 202 BC–AD 8 / Eastern Han 25–220）
- 大时代：Qin–Han
- 都城：Chang'an → Luoyang
- 特征：Unification settled, the Silk Road opened, Confucianism made orthodox, and the dynasty's name became the ethnonym. Major emperors, ~420 yrs.
- 总结：Forged the core Han identity and the centralized model; “Han” became the name of the people.

### 帝王
- Han Gaozu (Liu Bang)（Founding）：202–195 BC，在位约 8 年，庙号 Taizu，谥号 Gao。Rose from commoner origins, founded the dynasty
- Han Huidi (Liu Ying)：195–188 BC，在位约 7 年，谥号 Hui。Empress Lü in power
- Empress Lü (regent)（Regent）：188–180 BC，在位约 8 年。De facto ruler; mother of the Wen and Jing emperors
- Han Wendi (Liu Heng)（Wen–Jing era）：180–157 BC，在位约 23 年，庙号 Taizong，谥号 Wen。The Wen–Jing era of good rule; rest and recuperation
- Han Jingdi (Liu Qi)（Wen–Jing era）：157–141 BC，在位约 16 年，谥号 Jing。Reined in the vassals, quelled the Seven Kingdoms revolt
- Han Wudi (Liu Che)（Heyday）：141–87 BC，在位约 54 年，庙号 Shizong，谥号 Wu。Expanded into the Western Regions; a mighty Han
- Han Zhaodi (Liu Fuling)：87–74 BC，在位约 13 年，谥号 Zhao。Huoguang as regent
- Han Xuandi (Liu Xun)：74–49 BC，在位约 25 年，庙号 Zhongzong，谥号 Xuan。The Xiaoxuan era of good rule
- Han Yuandi (Liu Shi)：49–33 BC，在位约 16 年，庙号 Gaozong，谥号 Yuan。Confucianism made sole orthodoxy
- Han Chengdi (Liu Ao)：33–7 BC，在位约 26 年，谥号 Cheng。The Wang clan (empress's kin) rose
- Han Aidi (Liu Xin)：7–1 BC，在位约 6 年，谥号 Ai。Entrusted the orphan to Wang Mang
- Han Pingdi (Liu Kan)：1 BC–AD 5，在位约 6 年，谥号 Ping。Wang Mang as regent
- Ruzi Ying (Liu Ying)（Regency）：6–8，在位约 2 年。Wang Mang as regent; the Han mandate near its end
- Wang Mang (Xin)（Usurped Han）：9–23，在位约 15 年，谥号 Xin。Founded the Xin; revived antiquity in reform
- Gengshi Emperor (Liu Xuan)（Restored Han）：23–25，在位约 2 年。Set up by the Green Forest army; later killed by the Red Eyebrows
- Han Guangwudi (Liu Xiu)（Eastern Han founder）：25–57，在位约 33 年，庙号 Shizu，谥号 Guangwu。The Guangwu Restoration
- Han Mingdi (Liu Zhuang)：57–75，在位约 18 年，谥号 Ming。Buddhism entered the Central Plains
- Han Zhangdi (Liu Da)：75–88，在位约 13 年，谥号 Zhang。The Ming–Zhang era of good rule
- Han Hedi (Liu Zhao)：88–105，在位约 17 年，谥号 He。Dou Xian crushed the Xiongnu
- Han Shangdi (Liu Long)：105–106，在位约 1 年，谥号 Shang。Enthroned over a hundred days old; reigned eight months
- Han Andi (Liu Hu)：106–125，在位约 19 年，谥号 An。Struggle between empress's kin and eunuchs
- Han Shundi (Liu Bao)：125–144，在位约 19 年，谥号 Shun。Eunuchs gained the upper hand
- Han Huandi (Liu Zhi)：147–167，在位约 21 年，谥号 Huan。The Partisan Proscriptions
- Han Lingdi (Liu Hong)：168–189，在位约 22 年，谥号 Ling。The Yellow Turban Rebellion
- Han Xiandi (Liu Xie)（Last ruler）：189–220，在位约 31 年，谥号 Xian。A puppet; the Han fell

### 人才
- Civil Officials：Xiao He（Chancellor at the dawn of Han）；Zhang Liang（Strategist）；Dong Zhongshu（Made Confucianism sole orthodoxy）；Huoguang（Regent minister）
- Generals：Han Xin（The god of war）；Wei Qing（Struck the Xiongnu）；Huo Qubing（Sealed the Langjuxu summit）；Ban Chao（Cast aside the pen for the sword; opened the Western Regions）
- Thinkers & Men of Letters：Sima Qian（Records of the Grand Historian）；Cai Lun（Improved papermaking）；Zhang Heng（Seismograph and armillary sphere）；Hua Tuo（Famed physician; the mafeisan anesthetic）

### 制度
- Parallel princedoms → the Enfeoffment Reduction Edict
- Dismissed the Hundred Schools, upheld Confucianism alone
- The recommendation system
- The Silk Road
- State monopoly on salt and iron
- Equal-transport and price-stabilization

### 三维（政治 · 经济 · 文化）
- Politics：After parallel princedoms, the Enfeoffment Reduction Edict reined in vassals and centralized power；The recommendation system selected officials; empress's kin and eunuchs took turns monopolizing power
- Economy：Opened the Silk Road; state salt-iron monopoly, equal-transport and price-stabilization；Advances in agriculture, textiles and iron-smelting
- Culture：Dismissed the Hundred Schools, upheld Confucianism alone；Records of the Grand Historian and Book of Han survived; Cai Lun improved papermaking; Buddhism arrived

## Xin Dynasty（AD 9–23）
- 大时代：Qin–Han
- 都城：Chang'an (Everlasting Peace)
- 特征：Wang Mang usurped Han to found Xin, imposing antiquarian reforms (land, coinage, offices); the strife soon boiled over, and Xin fell to the Lulin and Chimei, after which Liu Xiu restored Han. Major figures, 14 yrs.
- 总结：A brief reforming interlude between two Hans; its institutional experiments influenced later ages.

### 人才
- Others：Wang Mang（Usurped Han to found Xin; antiquarian reforms）；Liu Xin（Old-text Confucianism; mathematics）

### 制度
- The Wang Tian land system
- Coinage reform
- Five Balances and Six Controls

### 三维（政治 · 经济 · 文化）
- Politics：The kinsman Wang Mang reigned as regent then usurped Han, reforming by antiquarian model；Scholar-officials ruled; ideals detached from reality
- Economy：Wang Tian and private-bondage banned sale; coinage changed often, harassing life；Five Balances and Six Controls — state monopolies that deepened strife

## Three Kingdoms（220–280）
- 大时代：Three Kingdoms & Divisions
- 都城：Wei, Shu and Wu
- 特征：Wei, Shu and Wu stood in a tripartite balance; a stage of heroes and stratagems. The three coexisted; reign years are not summed (counted by period).
- 总结：An age of heroes; Romance of the Three Kingdoms made it household lore.

### 帝王
- Cao Pi, Emperor Wen of Wei（Wei）：220–226，在位约 6 年，谥号 Wen。Usurped Han, declared himself emperor
- Cao Rui, Emperor Ming of Wei（Wei）：226–239，在位约 13 年，谥号 Ming。Massive building works
- Cao Huan, Emperor Yuan of Wei（Late Wei）：260–265，在位约 5 年。Abdicated to Jin
- Liu Bei, Emperor Zhaolie of Shu Han（Shu）：221–223，在位约 2 年，谥号 Zhaolie。Sought to revive the Han
- Liu Shan, Later Lord of Shu（Shu）：223–263，在位约 40 年，谥号 Huai。“Found new pleasures and forgot Shu”
- Sun Quan, Great Emperor of Wu（Wu）：222–252，在位约 30 年，谥号 Da。Held the Jiangdong region
- Sun Hao, Last Emperor of Wu（Late Wu）：264–280，在位约 16 年。Tyrannical; Wu fell

### 人才
- Civil Officials：Cao Cao（The martial Duke of Wei, a towering talent）；Zhuge Liang（Chancellor of Shu; devoted unto death）；Sima Yi（Powerful minister of Wei）；Lu Xun（Great general of Wu）
- Generals：Guan Yu（The Martial Saint）；Zhang Fei（A fierce general）；Zhao Yun（Zhao Zilong of Changshan）；Zhou Yu（Commander of Eastern Wu）

### 制度
- The tuntian (military-agriculture) system
- The nine-rank system (Wei)

### 三维（政治 · 经济 · 文化）
- Politics：Wei, Shu and Wu stood tripartite; civil and military rule fused；Wei used the nine-rank system; the gentry's power rose
- Economy：The tuntian system restored production; Jiangnan developed faster；Shu brocade, Wu shipbuilding and overseas trade flourished
- Culture：Jian'an literature, solemn and stirring；Calligraphy (Zhong Yao) and strategy culture thrived

## Jin（Western Jin 265–316 / Eastern Jin 317–420）
- 大时代：Three Kingdoms & Divisions
- 都城：Luoyang → Jiankang
- 特征：Brief unity then long division; the gentry aristocracy flourished. Major emperors, ~155 yrs.
- 总结：Unity broke apart at once, opening a long north–south standoff.

### 帝王
- Sima Yan, Emperor Wu of Jin（Founding）：265–290，在位约 25 年，庙号 Shizu，谥号 Wu。Destroyed Wu, unified
- Sima Zhong, Emperor Hui of Jin：290–306，在位约 16 年，谥号 Hui。The War of the Eight Princes; “Why not eat meat?”
- Sima Chi, Emperor Huai of Jin：306–311，在位约 5 年，谥号 Huai。Captured in the Yongjia turmoil

## Ming（1368–1644）
- 大时代：Ming–Qing
- 都城：Nanjing → Beijing
- 特征：Han restored unification; the sea ban coexisted with Zheng He's voyages. Major emperors, 276 yrs.
- 总结：Autocracy intensified yet a vibrant commodity economy; big moves by land and sea.

## Qing（1636–1912）
- 大时代：Ming–Qing
- 都城：Beijing

- 总结：The final chapter of imperial rule, turning from prosperity to decline as it entered the world system.

## 历史大事记
- 前2070 年：Founding of the Xia（Xia）— Qi, son of Yu, established hereditary rule — the start of “family rule” (approx.).
- 前1600 年：Tang of Shang overthrows Xia（Shang）— Tang founds Shang; oracle bone script is China's earliest mature writing.
- 前1046 年：Battle of Muye · Rise of Zhou（Zhou）— King Wu defeats Zhou, Shang falls and Zhou rises, enfeoffment begins.
- 前841 年：Gonghe Regency（Zhou）— Commoner revolt; the start of China's precise chronology.
- 前770 年：King Ping moves east（Zhou）— Eastern Zhou begins; the Spring and Autumn contending lords opens.
- 前221 年：Qin unifies the six states（Qin）— Ying Zheng claims the imperial title; abolishes feudalism for commanderies.
- 前209 年：Uprising of Chen Sheng & Wu Guang（Qin）— The first large-scale peasant uprising in Chinese history.
- 前202 年：Founding of the Han（Han）— Liu Bang becomes emperor, opening the four-century Han.
- 前138 年：Zhang Qian reaches the Western Regions（Han）— The Silk Road is opened.
- 105 年：Cai Lun improves papermaking（Han）— Paper spreads; a revolution in knowledge transmission.
- 184 年：Yellow Turban Rebellion（Han）— Eastern Han shaken; heroes rise across the land.
- 200 年：Battle of Guandu（Three Kingdoms）— Cao Cao defeats Yuan Shao, laying the base to unite the north.
- 208 年：Battle of Red Cliffs（Three Kingdoms）— Sun–Liu coalition breaks Cao Cao; the tripartite pattern forms.
- 220 年：Cao Pi takes the throne（Three Kingdoms）— Eastern Han ends; the Three Kingdoms era begins.
- 280 年：Western Jin destroys Wu（Jin）— Ends the Three Kingdoms split; brief unification.
- 317 年：Gentry cross south（Jin）— Eastern Jin founded; the gentry clans migrate south.
- 383 年：Battle of Fei River（Jin）— Eastern Jin wins outnumbered; Former Qin collapses.
- 589 年：Sui destroys Chen, reunifies（Sui）— Ends the north–south split; realm restored to unity.
- 605 年：Grand Canal dug（Sui）— Links north and south; shapes a millennium of grain transport.
- 618 年：Founding of the Tang（Tang）— Li Yuan founds Tang; China enters its golden age.
- 626 年：Xuanwu Gate Incident（Tang）— Li Shimin takes the throne, opening the Zhenguan era.
- 755 年：An–Shi Rebellion（Tang）— The turning point of Tang's decline from prosperity.
- 907 年：Tang falls · Five Dynasties begin（Five Dynasties & Ten Kingdoms）— Zhu Wen replaces Tang; the Central Plains enter the Five Dynasties.
- 1004 年：Treaty of Chanyuan（Song）— Song–Liao peace; a long standoff pattern forms.
- 1127 年：Jingkang Incident（Song）— Jin destroys Northern Song; the Song court flees south.
- 1206 年：Genghis Khan founds Mongolia（Yuan）— Mongols rise, beginning conquest and expansion.
- 1279 年：Battle of Yaishan（Yuan）— Southern Song falls; Yuan unifies the realm.
- 1405 年：Zheng He's voyages（Ming）— Voyages to the Western Oceans, on an unprecedented scale.
- 1616 年：Nurhaci founds Later Jin（Qing）— The Qing precursor rises in the Northeast.
- 1644 年：Ming falls · Qing enters the pass（Qing）— Dynastic change; Qing settles the Central Plains.
- 1840 年：Opium War（Qing）— The beginning of modern Chinese history.
- 1912 年：Qing emperor abdicates（Qing）— Imperial rule ends; two millennia of dynasty history closes.

## 四大发明
- 📜 Papermaking（Eastern Han，Cai Lun (improved, 105 AD)）：Using bark, hemp, rag and such to improve papermaking; cheap, accessible paper became a key vehicle for knowledge. 世界影响：Reached Arabia in the 8th c., then Europe, advancing world civilization.
- 🖨️ Printing（Sui–Tang → Northern Song，Woodblock (Sui–Tang) · Bi Sheng invented movable type (Northern Song)）：Woodblock printing began in Sui–Tang; in the Northern Song Bi Sheng made clay movable type, hugely boosting book replication. 世界影响：Accelerated knowledge diffusion, preluding European movable type.
- 💥 Gunpowder（Tang → Song，Tang alchemists · Song military use）：First recorded in Tang alchemical texts; used in war by late Tang; widely used in Song weapons. 世界影响：Changed warfare; spread west to Europe via Mongols in the 13th c.
- 🧭 Compass（Warring States → Northern Song，“Sinan” of Warring States · used for navigation in Northern Song）：The Warring States had the pointing tool “Sinan”; the Northern Song made an artificial magnetized compass for sea navigation. 世界影响：Supported ocean voyages and the Age of Discovery, reshaping world geography.
